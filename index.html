A LITTLE OCTOBER GUIDE

What to choose
this season.

October in Canada is made for gathering, sharing and bringing home something warm, fruity or just a little spooky.

OCTOBER · CANADIAN THANKSGIVING

Gather around the table

A time for gratitude, family and a generous dessert. Choose a slice of pumpkin pie with a scoop of ice cream.

Best pick: Pumpkin pie + scoop

OCTOBER · APPLE HARVEST

Celebrate the orchard

Apple harvest is the season’s sweetest tradition: crisp fruit, warm pastry and jars of bright jam to take home.

Best pick: Apple pie + jam

OCTOBER 31 · HALLOWEEN

Keep it sweet and a little spooky

For costumes, doorstep treats and late-night movie snacks, choose something colourful, fruity and easy to share.

Best pick: Berry jam + fresh pie slice
